What we do

Greentastic connects farmers and makers directly with their customers: sellers list their products, customers browse, order and pick them up or get them delivered.

Cutting out the middlemen gives farmers a fair price and customers fresh products for less.

This is a demo application used as an example for observability with OpenTelemetry.
